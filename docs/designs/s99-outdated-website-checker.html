Is your website out of date?

Check up to ten public pages for dates, prices and promotions that may have passed. Each result shows the exact line and why it was flagged.

We read your homepage and up to nine more public pages. Anything after a ? or # in the address is left out, so links with tokens stay private.

Idle Action remains disabled until an address is present.
Scanning Scanning your pages. This can take up to 20 seconds.
Refused or failed Fixed server message in the existing destructive Alert.
Success Load the saved report as a new document.

What it checks

  • Copyright years behind this year
  • Past dates still described as upcoming
  • Year-stamped prices, rates, menus and schedules
  • Past-year and out-of-season promotions
  • Old “last updated” labels, shown as information

What it leaves alone

  • Blog dates without future or deadline wording
  • “Since 2015” and company history
  • Numeric slash dates such as 03/04/2025
  • Non-English and browser-added copy

A warning means “review this line”, not “the page is wrong”.

Questions

Does every old date mean the page is outdated?

No. Nearby wording must still present it as current, upcoming or promotional.

How are month-only dates treated?

A month is not past until its final UTC day has ended.

Does it read text added by JavaScript?

No. It reads the HTML the server sends.
