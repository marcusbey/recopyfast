2 — Claim confirmation dialog

Opens on "Claim subdomain" from the Success state above. This is the actual commit point — the irreversibility warning gets its full weight here, and the primary action stays disabled until the agency types the exact hostname, the same deliberate-friction pattern products use for claiming a permanent username or a custom domain.

Claim acme.recopyfa.st?

This can't be undone

acme.recopyfa.st becomes yours permanently the moment you confirm. It can't be released, changed, or reassigned later. Every snippet issued from now on will point here, and those snippets get pasted onto your clients' sites — pages we don't control. Even if you cancel the Agency plan later, this subdomain keeps serving whatever it's still installed on.
