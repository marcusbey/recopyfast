Splits must add up to 100% — currently 70%. Control gets the remainder automatically once variant splits are valid.

"Saved" confirmation (green check, `--dur-fast` fade) is the visible signal that `saveEdit` now persists — the fix for the review-step-edits-are-lost defect. Split input is `Input` + a right-side "%" label, not an icon-in-input and not a slider (no slider exists in `src/components/ui/`, and typed values are more precise than a drag for a value that must sum exactly to 100). "Next" stays disabled until the total is exactly 100%.
