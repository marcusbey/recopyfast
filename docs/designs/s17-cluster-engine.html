FAQ

Common questions

Is RecopyFast a real alternative to TinaCMS?⌄

For teams whose priority is a git-backed content model and structured schemas, TinaCMS is the better fit — that's stated plainly above. RecopyFast is the better fit when the site already exists and the only thing that needs to change is who can edit the words.

Can I keep my git-based content workflow?⌄

Yes — RecopyFast doesn't touch your repository. It edits the rendered page directly, so your git history, branches and PR review process are unaffected.

What does TinaCMS do better?⌄

Git-native version control, structured content schemas, and self-hosting at zero license cost — see "Where TinaCMS wins" above.

Do my clients need a GitHub account?⌄

No. RecopyFast's editing grants are email-based and don't require any account at all — not GitHub, not RecopyFast's own.

How long does switching take?⌄

Typically under five minutes for the install itself — see the migration note above. There's no data export/import step because your site's content never left the site.

Ready to see it on your own site?

No migration, no schema to design — paste one tag and start editing.
