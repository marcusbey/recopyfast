s66a — App design tokens and panels

The redesigned site-registered panel and Share preview link dialog, and the primitives they are built from. Every value comes from docs/design-system.md (s66a revision). Containers are square, controls have 2 px corners, borders are 1 px, and only floating surfaces cast a shadow. Every dialog has a single scroll container: its body.

  2. 2

    Paste it before </body>

    Paste it into your theme's footer.php, immediately before the closing </body> tag.

    No access to theme files? A header-and-footer snippet plugin drops it in the same place, and survives theme updates.

    For React or Next.js, load the generated tag after the page has hydrated. In Next.js, next/script with strategy="afterInteractive" is one option; preserve every generated attribute and value.

    The widget has no complete lifecycle for client-side route changes. Verify direct loads, internal navigation, Back, reload, and returning to an edited page.

    Paste it before the closing </body> tag of every page you want to be editable.

    Sharing one footer include across pages means pasting it once; otherwise each page needs its own copy.

  3. 3

    Open your site — text is editable by itself

    The widget finds headings, paragraphs, list items, table cells, labels, buttons and images by itself — no markup changes.
