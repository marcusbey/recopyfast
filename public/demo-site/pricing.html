FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can't find what you're looking for? Contact our support team.

Can I change plans at any time?

Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. When upgrading, you'll be charged the prorated difference. When downgrading, you'll receive credit toward future bills.

What happens if I exceed my bandwidth limit?

We'll never cut off your traffic. If you exceed your limit, we'll reach out to discuss upgrading your plan. We also offer bandwidth add-ons if you need occasional spikes.

Do you offer discounts for startups?

Yes! We have a startup program that offers 50% off for the first year. Contact our sales team with your company details to see if you qualify.

Is there a free trial?

All paid plans come with a 14-day free trial. No credit card required to start. You'll only be charged if you decide to continue after the trial period.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express) and can arrange wire transfers or invoicing for Enterprise customers.

Can I get a refund?

If you're not satisfied within the first 30 days, we offer a full refund, no questions asked. After that, we provide prorated refunds on a case-by-case basis.

Still have questions?

Our team is here to help. Schedule a call with our sales team to discuss your specific needs and get a custom quote.
