About NovaTech

We're on a mission to democratize cloud infrastructure

Founded in 2019, NovaTech has grown from a small team of developers frustrated with existing cloud solutions to a global platform serving thousands of companies across 150+ countries.

Our Story

Built by developers, for developers

We started NovaTech because we believed cloud infrastructure should be accessible to everyone, not just large enterprises with dedicated DevOps teams.

Our founders experienced firsthand the complexity of deploying and scaling applications. Configuration files, server management, load balancing, SSL certificates—it was overwhelming. We knew there had to be a better way.

Today, NovaTech handles all that complexity behind the scenes, letting developers focus on what they do best: building great products. Our platform processes over 50 million deployments per month and serves trillions of requests globally.
